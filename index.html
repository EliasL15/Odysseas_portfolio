About

Odysseas Pantazis is a composer, sound designer, and emerging filmmaker whose work spans music composition, experimental cinema, and narrative sound. Based in London with roots in Cyprus, his creative practice explores the tension between structured composition and abstract sonic environments, often shaped through field recordings, analog processing, and unconventional forms.

Currently studying Film Music at Guildhall School of Music & Drama, he works across scoring, sound design, and collaborative film-based projects. While his main focus remains in musical experimentation, he has increasingly incorporated moving image into his work as a way to explore sonic ideas through film.
